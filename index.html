Privacy Policy — One Lane Left

Effective date: 3 September 2026

1. Introduction

One Lane Left is published by Kinetic Works ("Kinetic Works", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use One Lane Left on Android.

One Lane Left is a game. We do not provide player accounts or require you to log in to play. The game saves some game and settings information on your device. Some online services may be used for analytics, advertising, or purchases when those features are enabled in a release.

2. Information We Process

Information stored locally by the game

The game stores the following information on your device using app-local storage:

  • game progress such as high score, virtual currency total, and daily-mission state;
  • game settings, including sound, music, haptics, graphics, camera-motion, and usage-analytics choices;
  • a local flag recording a confirmed Remove Ads entitlement; and
  • a locally generated random installation identifier used by the daily-mission save system. The audited project does not send this identifier to Kinetic Works.

This information is used to operate the game, restore local gameplay state, and apply your settings. It is not an account profile and does not contain a player name, email address, contacts, or payment-card information.

The audited project does not request precise device location or access contacts, photos/media, health data, or direct payment-card credentials. Advertising providers may still infer approximate location from IP address when advertising is enabled, as described below.

Usage analytics

The game includes Unity Analytics. The game initializes Unity Services and records usage analytics only when you have explicitly enabled Usage Analytics through the in-game analytics choice or Settings control.

When enabled, the project is designed to send game and technical event data such as app readiness, app foreground/background state, screen views, button actions, run number, run duration, distance, score, virtual cash, gameplay outcome, selected graphics tier, and performance measurements. These events include app version/build information and are sent to Unity Technologies through Unity Analytics.

The project contains a bounded crash-context metadata writer for technical game state. The current audited project has Unity's Crash Report API disabled and does not include a separate Cloud Diagnostics package. If crash reporting is enabled in a future release, Unity or the relevant diagnostics provider may process crash and device information under its own privacy notice.

3. Advertising and Advertising Identifiers

The project includes Unity LevelPlay mediation, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads mediation components, and the Google Mobile Ads/AdMob adapter configured for Partner Bidding. The current audited configuration has live ads disabled. It also contains development-only LevelPlay testing controls; those controls are not included in normal Release builds.

If an ad-supported release is enabled, advertising SDKs and their demand partners may process technical and advertising-related information. This can include an advertising identifier, device and network information, IP address, app and ad interaction data, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad-performance data, and consent signals. This information may be used to deliver, measure, limit, secure, and personalize ads where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements.

Advertising identifiers and the exact data practices of advertising partners are controlled by the applicable SDKs and their privacy notices. Kinetic Works does not receive the advertising identifier as a game feature.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

The game has a separate Usage Analytics choice in its first-run/settings experience. That choice controls project analytics submission; it is not an advertising-consent substitute.

For an ad-supported release, Kinetic Works intends to use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another supported certified consent-management flow where required, including for applicable users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. The resulting consent or privacy choice must be provided to LevelPlay and AdMob before ads initialize, and users must be offered any required Privacy, cookie-settings, or privacy-options entry point.

The current codebase contains the production boundary for receiving an ad privacy state, but a Google UMP integration has not yet been confirmed in the audited project. Until that workflow is implemented and validated for a release, this policy does not state that a production advertising consent flow is active.

5. In-App Purchases

The project includes Unity IAP support for Google Play purchases, including a potential non-consumable Remove Ads product. The current audited configuration has IAP disabled and no active Google Play product is confirmed.

If purchases are enabled, payment processing and purchase verification are handled by Google Play and Unity IAP. Kinetic Works does not collect or store payment-card numbers. The game may store a local entitlement flag so that a confirmed Remove Ads purchase can be recognized on the device.

6. How Information Is Used

Depending on which features are enabled in a release, information is used to operate the game and retain local settings and progress; understand game usage and technical performance when Usage Analytics is enabled; provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising when advertising is enabled; process and restore eligible in-app purchases when purchases are enabled; and meet applicable legal, security, and fraud-prevention obligations.

7. Data Sharing

Kinetic Works does not operate an account system or a Kinetic Works cloud-save service in the audited project. We may share or make information available to service providers only as needed for enabled services:

  • Unity Technologies for Unity Analytics, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and Unity IAP;
  • Google for Google Play purchase processing and, when ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads/AdMob; and
  • advertising demand partners available through LevelPlay mediation when ads are enabled.

Those providers may process information under their own policies and may use subprocessors or operate internationally. We do not directly sell personal information for money. Whether a particular disclosure is treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under a specific law depends on the facts, enabled partners, and applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

App-local game and settings data normally remains on your device until it is changed, reset by available in-game controls, cleared through Android settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled. Uninstalling normally removes the app's local data, but Android backup, device-management, or platform behavior can affect what remains outside the app.

Kinetic Works does not provide account deletion because the audited game has no account system. Information processed by Unity, Google, or advertising partners is retained according to their applicable policies and operational requirements.

For a privacy inquiry or deletion request relating to Kinetic Works-controlled information, contact us at kineticworks.games@gmail.com. Please do not send payment-card details in a request.

9. Children's Privacy

One Lane Left is not intended as a child-directed product. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a player account, because the game has no account or login system. Advertising, analytics, and purchase providers may have their own rules for minors and child-directed apps. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Kinetic Works directly, contact kineticworks.games@gmail.com.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the services we operate, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party providers apply their own security practices to information they process.

11. International Processing

Unity, Google, and advertising partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy notices describe their processing and transfer practices.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request information about personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or to withdraw consent where consent is used. You may also be able to change advertising choices through the consent/privacy controls made available in the app or through device/platform settings.

To make a request concerning Kinetic Works-controlled information, contact kineticworks.games@gmail.com. We may need information necessary to verify and respond to a request. Requests concerning Unity, Google, or advertising-partner data may also need to be directed to the relevant provider.
